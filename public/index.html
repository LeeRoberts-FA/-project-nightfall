HOW TO PLAY

1. Create a squad and send the six-character code or invite link to a friend. Two to four real people may join from different devices.

2. Deploy together. The squad leader can start co-op once at least two players have joined. Solo training can be started immediately.

3. Fight your mission waves (three waves in Titan Raid, five in Blackwater Outbreak), then defeat your mission boss. Coordinate fire and use class abilities.

4. Upgrade your character. Eliminating enemies earns XP. Each new level grants a point you can spend on Damage, Armor, or Mobility (five ranks each).

WIN: Defeat the mission boss (Warden Titan in Titan Raid, Harvester in Outbreak). LOSE: Every operative is down with no reinforcements remaining. On Veteran, co-op squads start with four reinforcements and solo with six. Recruit adds two; Nightmare removes two. Reinforcements respawn fallen operatives after four seconds.

CONTROLS

Desktop: WASD or arrows to move. Aim with mouse, click/hold or SPACE to fire. Q / C rotate the camera. Movement follows your view. R reloads the 30-round magazine (also reloads automatically when empty). E uses ability. U opens upgrades. Numbers 1/2/3 purchase upgrades. H opens rules.

Aim assist: ON automatically targets and fires at the nearest hostile. Turn it off for manual aim. The camera stays fixed while you aim.

Mobile: Left joystick moves. Right joystick aims and fires. Use the VIEW arrows to rotate the camera, plus ABILITY and UPGRADES buttons. The contact radar shows threats outside your view.

Class abilities: Assault fires a five-shot burst; Medic restores 65 HP to nearby allies; Heavy recovers 50 HP; Scout dashes forward. Each recharges in 16 seconds. XP lasts for the current mission; redeploying resets your build.
