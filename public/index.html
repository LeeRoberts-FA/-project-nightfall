HOW TO PLAY

1. Create a squad and send the six-character code or invite link to a friend. Two to four real people may join from different devices.

2. Deploy together. The squad leader can start co-op once at least two players have joined. Solo training can be started immediately.

3. Fight your mission waves of enemy drones, then defeat the Warden Titan boss. Coordinate fire and use class abilities.

4. Upgrade your character. Eliminating enemies earns XP. Each new level grants a point you can spend on Damage, Armor, or Mobility (five ranks each).

WIN: Defeat the mission boss (Warden Titan in Titan Raid, Harvester in Outbreak). LOSE: Every operative is down with no reinforcements remaining. Co-op squads start with four reinforcements; solo training gives six.

CONTROLS

Desktop: WASD or arrows to move. Aim with mouse, click/hold or SPACE to fire. E uses ability. U opens upgrades. Numbers 1/2/3 purchase upgrades. H opens rules.

Mobile: Left joystick moves. Right joystick aims and fires. Use ABILITY and UPGRADES buttons.
